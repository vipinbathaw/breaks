# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 25, 2026

Breaks ("the app") is a break-reminder application developed by Vipin Bathaw.
This policy explains what information the app handles — in short: **none**.

## Data collection

The app collects **no data**. Specifically:

- No personal information is collected, stored, or transmitted
- No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking tools are integrated
- The app contains no advertising
- The app has **no network access permission at all** — it physically cannot
  send any information off your device

## Data storage

All settings you configure (intervals, active hours, theme preference) are
stored locally on your device using Android's DataStore API. This data never
leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

## Permissions

The app requests only the permissions required for its core function:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Notifications | To show you the break reminder |
| Alarms & reminders | To schedule reminders at precise times |
| Full-screen intent | To display full-screen reminders (optional, off by default) |
| Run at startup | To restore your reminder schedule after a device reboot |
| Vibration | For the optional reminder vibration |
| Ignore battery optimizations | Optional — prevents aggressive battery savers from delaying reminders |

None of these permissions access your personal data.

## Third-party services

The app does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or libraries that collect
data.

## Children

The app is a general-audience product and does not knowingly collect any data
from anyone, including children under 13.

Any changes to this policy will be published in this file with an updated
effective date.
